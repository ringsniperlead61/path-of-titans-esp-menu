## 🧩 About

**Path of Titans ESP Menu** is a curated list of Path of Titans open-source tools:

- **Quality-of-Life Helpers** — diet planner, growth tracker, quest notes

## ✨ Features

### ESP
- Creature Tracker — on-screen markers for nearby dinosaurs
- Name Plates — readable labels for wildlife and players
- Growth Notes — track growth stages and stat progress
- Stamina Reader — visualize stamina and hunger bars

### Wallhack
- Map Viewer — in-game map overlay with points of interest
- Loot Notes — mark food, water, and resource locations
- Compass Helper — bearing notes for navigation study

### Aimbot
- Diet Planner — organize herbivore and carnivore diets
- Quest Notes — track daily quest progress
- Config Presets — save and swap overlay layouts
